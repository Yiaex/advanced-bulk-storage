# ME Quantum Bulk Storage Cell

Combine registered tags, namespaces and exact item IDs. The editor accepts 64 tags by default. Bounded wildcards such as `#c:ingots/*` count each matching registered tag.

Defaults are **4096** types, **6** upgrade slots and **64 AE/t** of idle drain in a drive. Each complete item key supports up to 9,223,372,036,854,775,807 items. Use the cell in hand to configure it, then mount it in a powered, online AE2 Drive. Exact AE2 Cell Workbench partitions apply alongside the filter.

Hover over the cell to see the actual items and their quantities. Hold Shift to show more. The preview shows up to 9 or 36 item types by default, depending on GUI scale and available space. K, M, G, T, P, and E represent powers of 1,000. Use the cell and select Inventory, then hover over an entry to see its precise count.
